Legal & Data Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 — Version 2.1

At Vesture, we respect your individuality and protect your personal aesthetic. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard your data when using our digital wardrobe and styling platform.

(01)

Information We Collect

We only gather data necessary to personalize your styling experience and digitize your wardrobe:

  • Wardrobe Imagery: Garment photos uploaded for background removal and cataloging.
  • Styling Preferences: Aesthetic tags, color preferences, sizes, and occasion calendars.
  • Technical Information: Device type, browser environment, and session data for performance optimization.
(02)

How We Use Your Data

Your inputs directly drive your personal styling results:

  • Generating intelligent outfit pairings based on current weather and your events.
  • Building your digital wardrobe archive and tracking garment wear rotation.
  • Improving styling models without exposing private user content.
(03)

Garment Photos & Privacy

Your wardrobe photos belong strictly to you. Uploaded clothing images are processed securely and are never sold, rented, or licensed to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

(04)

Your Rights & Data Portability

You have the unconditional right to export your digitized wardrobe catalog, update your styling profile, or request full deletion of all associated images and account records at any time.
