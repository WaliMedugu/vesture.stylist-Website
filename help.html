How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about using Vesture, managing your wardrobe, and getting the most from your styling recommendations.

🚀 Getting Started
Vesture is an intelligent personal wardrobe assistant. You upload photos or videos of your outfits, and Vesture automatically names, tags, and organises them. You can then get personalised outfit recommendations from your own wardrobe — based on the occasion, weather, or mood.
Yes — Vesture's core features are completely free. Digitising your wardrobe, auto-tagging outfits, and receiving styling recommendations are all available at no cost. We may introduce optional premium features in the future.
Vesture is currently available on Android. iOS support is planned for a future release. You can download Vesture from the Google Play Store.
No account is required. Vesture is entirely local — your wardrobe data is stored on your device. There is no sign-up, login, or cloud account needed to use the app.
📸 Uploading Outfits
Tap the "+" button to add outfits. You can import photos from your gallery (multi-select supported) or upload a video — Vesture will extract individual outfit frames automatically.
When you upload a video, Vesture extracts frames at 1 frame per second and removes near-duplicate frames. You then review the remaining frames in a swipe interface — swipe right to keep, left to discard.
Each photo is automatically analysed, which returns a descriptive name and a set of relevant tags (fabric, colour, style, occasion, etc.). You can review and edit these before saving.
Yes. Vesture can accurately identify and name cultural garments including Agbadas, Senator wear, Kaftans, Saris, Kimonos, Hanboks, and many more from every region of the world.
✨ Styling & Recommendations
Tell Vesture what you need — "I have a business meeting today" or "something casual for a rainy Sunday" — and it selects the most relevant outfits from your wardrobe and assembles a fully coordinated look, slot by slot.
Tapping the "+" button in the chat screen opens a context panel where you can set the occasion (e.g., Office, Casual, Date Night) and weather condition. These help make recommendations more relevant to your day.
Every recommendation includes thumbs up/down feedback buttons. Your responses are stored in a personal style profile. Vesture uses this to boost colours and styles you love and avoid combinations you've rejected.
The WCF is a smart indexing algorithm that narrows your wardrobe down to the most relevant outfits before generating any recommendation. This keeps responses fast and relevant regardless of how many outfits you've uploaded.
👗 Managing Your Wardrobe
Open any outfit's detail view and tap "Save Worn Today". This logs the wear date and helps Vesture avoid over-recommending recently worn looks.
Yes. Tap any outfit to open the detail screen, then tap the name to edit it inline. Tags can be toggled on or off with a single tap. All changes are saved locally.
Yes. The home screen has a real-time search bar at the top and category filter pills below it. You can filter by tags, categories, or search by name to find any outfit instantly.
🔒 Privacy & Data
All wardrobe data — outfit photos, tags, names, wear history, and your style profile — is stored entirely on your device using SQLite. We do not operate servers that store your personal wardrobe data.
Selected outfit images and chat messages are sent to a third-party AI API for processing (auto-tagging and styling recommendations). Images are sent over encrypted HTTPS and are not stored by Vesture on any server.
No. We do not sell, share, or monetise any user data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
🛠 Troubleshooting
Check your internet connection first — the styling assistant requires an active connection. If connectivity is fine, close and reopen the app and try again. If the issue persists, please report the bug.
Video extraction is only available on iOS and Android, where hardware codecs are accessible. Desktop builds of Vesture do not support video import by design.
Use our Bug Report page to submit a detailed description of the issue. Including steps to reproduce and your Android version helps us fix it faster.
