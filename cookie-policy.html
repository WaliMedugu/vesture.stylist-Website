Storage & Cookie Usage

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 — Version 2.1

This Cookie Policy describes how Vesture uses browser storage and cookies to remember your styling sessions and ensure responsive website navigation.

(01)

What Are Cookies

Cookies and local storage tokens are small text files placed on your device to ensure smooth site navigation, preserve your active session, and deliver tailored styling recommendations.

(02)

Cookie Categories We Use

  • Strictly Necessary: Essential for interface stability, preloader state, and session routing.
  • Styling Preferences: Preserves your selected wardrobe view, filters, and display settings.
  • Performance: Lightweight diagnostics to maintain low latency during image and lookbook rendering.
(03)

Managing Your Preferences

You can control or clear cookies directly through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may slightly alter your visual navigation experience.
