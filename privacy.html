Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Blue Particle ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Vesture application ("the App") on Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and how it is used.

By downloading and using Vesture, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and discontinue use.

Data We Collect

Vesture collects only the minimum information necessary to provide its features. We categorise data as follows:

  • Wardrobe Photos: Images you upload from your device gallery or extracted from a video. These are stored locally on your device.
  • Outfit Metadata: Names, tags, categories, and wear dates associated with your outfits. Stored locally in a SQLite database on your device.
  • Style Preferences: Your thumbs up / thumbs down feedback on outfit recommendations. Stored locally on your device.
  • Chat Messages: Text prompts you type in the styling chat. These are sent to a third-party API to generate responses and are not stored by Vesture.
  • Selected Outfit Images (API calls): When generating a recommendation, selected outfit images may be sent to a third-party API for contextual analysis. These are transmitted over HTTPS and not stored by Vesture beyond the API call.

We do not collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, advertising identifiers, financial information, or any account credentials.

API Processing & Third-Party Services

Vesture uses a third-party AI API to power its auto-tagging and styling recommendation features. When you use these features, selected outfit images and/or text prompts are transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to this third-party provider.

Data transmitted to the API is processed in real-time to generate a response. Vesture does not receive, log, or store this data on its own servers. However, the third-party provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle data submitted to their API.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of the AI provider in use. We select providers with strong data handling commitments and contractual data protections.

Local Storage

All wardrobe data — including outfit photos, names, tags, wear history, and your style profile — is stored exclusively on your device using Android's local storage and SQLite. This data does not leave your device except as described in the API Processing section above.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling Vesture will permanently delete all locally stored wardrobe data. We do not maintain backups of your wardrobe on any server.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing or commercial purposes. Data is only shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Third-party API providers: As described above, limited data is sent to our AI provider solely to generate the features you request.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect our legal rights.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user data may be transferred. We will notify users via an in-app notice or App Store listing update before any transfer occurs.

Retention & Deletion

Because wardrobe data is stored locally, you control retention entirely. You can delete individual outfits, clear your wear history, or delete all data by clearing the App's data via Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.

Data transmitted during API calls (images and text prompts) is not retained by Vesture. Any retention on the part of our API providers is governed by their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Vesture is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete data. Because Vesture stores wardrobe data locally, you can exercise these rights directly within the App or via Android device settings.

For any data transmitted to our API providers, you may contact us to request deletion. We will make reasonable efforts to honour such requests, though we cannot guarantee deletion of data held by third-party providers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with a new effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will provide notice through the App or the Google Play Store listing. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions, privacy concerns, or data deletion requests, please reach out to our team at vesture.stylist@gmail.com. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 14 business days.
