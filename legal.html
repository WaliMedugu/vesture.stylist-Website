📋 Quick Summary

Your data stays on your device

Wardrobe data — outfits, photos, tags, history — is stored locally using SQLite. We don't run servers that hold your wardrobe.

API calls are limited & encrypted

Outfit images are sent over HTTPS to a third-party API only for tagging and styling. We do not store them on our servers.

We don't sell or share your data

No advertising, no data brokers, no monetisation of your wardrobe. Your personal data is not our product.

No account required

Vesture works entirely without registration. There is no username, password, or profile linked to you anywhere.

All documents effective 1 January 2025. Questions or legal notices? Email vesture.stylist@gmail.com.
