Summary

Vesture is designed to be a private-first application. The vast majority of data you create — your outfits, tags, preferences, and wear history — never leaves your device. A small, clearly defined subset of data is transmitted to third-party AI providers to power the app's core features. This page provides complete transparency about all data flows.

Data Shared with Third Parties

The only data transmitted externally is:

  • Outfit images — selected by the user or required for feature processing — transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to a third-party AI API. This occurs during auto-tagging and styling recommendation generation.
  • Text prompts entered in the styling chat, transmitted to the same AI API for response generation.

We do not share any data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics platforms, or any third party other than the AI provider described above.

Data We Do NOT Collect

The following types of data are never collected by Vesture:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any registration identity
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device advertising ID or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Financial information or payment data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Browsing history or web activity
  • Crash reports or diagnostics sent to Vesture (note: crash data may be collected by Google Play if permitted by your device settings)

Security Practices

  • All external data transmissions use HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • Wardrobe data stored locally uses Android's standard application storage sandbox, inaccessible to other apps.
  • No passwords or sensitive credentials are stored by the App.
  • We regularly review our integrations to ensure third-party providers meet acceptable security and privacy standards.

Data Deletion

Because wardrobe data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Deleting individual outfits within the App.
  • Going to Android Settings > Apps > Vesture > Storage > Clear Data to erase all local app data.
  • Uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data.

For data transmitted to our AI provider, deletion is governed by that provider's policies. We cannot guarantee deletion on their systems, but we do not retain a copy on our servers.

Questions about data? Email us at vesture.stylist@gmail.com or use our bug report page.
