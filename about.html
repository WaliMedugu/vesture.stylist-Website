Our Mission

Make every outfit
decision effortless

We believe getting dressed should be joyful, not stressful. Vesture combines intelligent wardrobe technology with a beautifully designed system to give you personalised styling advice from your own closet — every morning, every occasion, anywhere in the world.

✦ Intelligent ✓ Privacy-First 🌍 Globally Aware ♥ Free to Use
Our Values

What we stand for

🔒
Privacy by Design

Your wardrobe data lives locally on your device. We never sell, share, or monetise your personal clothing data. What's yours stays yours.

🌍
Cultural Inclusivity

Vesture understands fashion from every corner of the world — from Agbada and Senator wear in West Africa to Kimonos in Japan and Saris in South Asia.

⚡
Intelligent Efficiency

Our Wardrobe Contraction Filter (WCF) algorithm keeps recommendations fast and accurate — no matter how large your wardrobe grows.

💡
Continuous Learning

Every thumbs up and thumbs down you give teaches Vesture your personal taste. It gets smarter with every interaction.

Our Story

A wardrobe problem,
solved

Vesture started with a very relatable problem: standing in front of a full wardrobe and feeling like you have nothing to wear. The solution wasn't more clothes — it was a smarter way to use the ones you already own.

We built Vesture to be the styling assistant most people can't afford in real life — one that knows your wardrobe, understands your culture, adapts to the weather and season, and gives genuinely useful, personalized advice every single day.

Developed under Blue Particle, Vesture is designed with care for fashion lovers everywhere.
